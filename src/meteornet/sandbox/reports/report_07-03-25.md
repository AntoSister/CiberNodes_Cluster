## Machine Learning 
- Allow multi services for user task computation. 
- Develop and test mixed **Terretrial/Space Cloud** network.
- Adapt ML algorithms: 1) **Fuzzy Logic**, and 2) **Reinforcement Learning** to solve multi services task allocation and change their objective functions to optimize both **Radio** and **Computation** resources \[1\]: 
$$ \text{min} \sum_m (\alpha_1 \cdot T^{off}_{m,k} (\tau^{off}_i) + \alpha_2 \cdot E^{off}_{m,k}(\tau^{off}_i)) $$ 
- Develop and adapt to multi service problem, the **Maximum Capacity Allocation** strategy used in \[1\].
